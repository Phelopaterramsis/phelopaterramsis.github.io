# Phelopater Ramsis

**Live site: [phelopaterramsis.github.io](https://phelopaterramsis.github.io)**

Personal website of Phelopater Ramsis, M.S. student in Electrical & Computer Engineering at the University of Florida, working on AI and optimization for energy systems.

The site is an interactive "world" rather than a static résumé: a globe that flies through the journey from Egypt to Nebraska to Florida, a lab of research projects, and an illustrated character that walks through each section.
